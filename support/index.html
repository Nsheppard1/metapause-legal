About MetaPause

MetaPause is a wellness-tracking app for logging symptoms, meals, and lifestyle patterns during perimenopause and menopause. It provides educational information and pattern recognition only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Seek help from a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns.

Subscriptions and renewal

MetaPause offers monthly auto-renewing subscriptions for additional digital features. The price and renewal period are shown before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple App Store or Google Play account when you confirm. Your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel before the store's renewal deadline.

Cancel or manage a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by the store where you purchased them. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, select MetaPause, and choose Cancel Subscription. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, select MetaPause, and choose Cancel subscription. Deleting MetaPause does not cancel a subscription. Cancellation stops future renewals; paid access normally continues through the end of the current billing period.

Refunds

Apple and Google process charges and decide refund eligibility for purchases made through their stores. MetaPause cannot directly issue an app-store refund. For an Apple purchase, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. For a Google Play purchase, use Google Play's order history or refund help. Refunds or charge reversals may remove paid access after the store reports the change.

Restore a purchase

Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple or Google account used to subscribe, then use Restore Purchases from the MetaPause subscription screen. If access is not restored, restart the app and try again after confirming that the subscription is active in your store account.
