Privacy Policy

How MetaPause handles health and wellness information, AI processing, and subscription data.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Information we collect

MetaPause collects information you choose to enter, including symptom check-ins (such as energy, hunger, cravings, sleep quality, brain fog, mood, hot flashes, and stress), meal descriptions and timing, profile information such as your name and health goals, and notification preferences.

Health and wellness data

Your health and wellness entries are stored locally on your device using secure local storage. MetaPause does not maintain a server-side copy of your personal health log. You can view your data in the app, export it from Profile settings, or permanently remove it with the Clear All Data option. Uninstalling the app also removes locally stored app data, subject to your device platform's backup behavior.

How we use information

We use the information you provide to show your history, identify patterns, generate personalized educational insights and daily actions, prepare weekly reports, suggest wellness experiments, and operate and improve the app.

AI-powered features

When you request an AI-powered insight or report, an anonymized summary of relevant symptom and meal data may be sent to our AI service provider for processing. MetaPause does not include your name or other direct identifiers in that request. AI-generated content is educational and is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. You may choose not to use AI-powered features.

Subscriptions and billing providers

RevenueCat receives an app-specific user identifier and purchase, subscription, and entitlement information so MetaPause can unlock paid features and restore purchases. Apple processes App Store purchases and refunds under Apple's privacy policy. Google processes Google Play purchases and refunds under Google's privacy policy. MetaPause does not receive your full payment-card details for app-store purchases.

Sharing and sale of data

MetaPause does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with service providers as described above, when required by law, or when necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of MetaPause and its users. We may use aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably identify you for research, analytics, and product improvement.

Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. Health data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Anonymized summaries sent for AI processing are not retained by MetaPause after the requested processing is complete. RevenueCat, Apple, and Google retain transaction records according to their own policies and legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can access, export, or delete your locally stored information in the app and can stop using AI-powered features. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. Use the support options below to ask a privacy question or make a request. We may need enough information to verify and respond to the request, but do not send health details in a support request.

Children's privacy

MetaPause is intended for adults and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that a child has provided personal information, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as MetaPause changes. We will revise the date above and provide an in-app notice when a change is material.

MetaPause provides educational information only and does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
